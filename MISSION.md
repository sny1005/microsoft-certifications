# Mission: Pass Microsoft Exam AB-100 — Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect

## Why
The user is a senior data scientist who builds AI solutions on Azure in a corporate environment and has applied for the AB-100 certification. Passing validates architect-level breadth across Microsoft's business AI stack — Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Copilot Studio, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Azure AI Foundry — not just the code-first depth they already have.

## Success looks like
- Scores ≥ 700 on AB-100 within 1–2 months
- Can make confident platform decisions: when to use prebuilt agents, extend Microsoft 365 Copilot, build in Copilot Studio, or go code-first in Azure AI Foundry
- Can design ALM, security, governance, monitoring, and testing strategies for agentic business solutions
- Can produce an ROI / build-buy-extend analysis for a proposed AI solution

## Constraints
- ~5+ hours per week of study time
- Exam window: 1–2 months
- Strong on Azure AI Foundry and IaaS/PaaS (vnet, Container Apps) — don't re-teach
- Practice style: mix of scenario quizzes and case-study walkthroughs

## Out of scope
- Deep IaaS/PaaS infrastructure (virtual networks, Container Apps internals) — already known
- Non-Microsoft agent frameworks (LangChain etc.) except where exam-relevant protocols (MCP, A2A) intersect
